Privacy policy

Novko app · BITLOCK CONSULTING · effective from 26 November 2026

HomeTerms of useHrvatski

In short

Novko has no user accounts and no server of its own. Your data (balances, income, expenses, projects, debtors, notes, receipt photos) is stored only on your phone. We don't receive it, see it or sell it. The app has no ads and no usage tracking.

What data the app uses

  • Data you enter – amounts, descriptions, categories, names of people you lend to or borrow from, notes. It stays on the device.
  • Camera – only when you start it, to photograph receipts (and, in the Croatian-language version, to scan the QR code on Croatian fiscal receipts). Photos are saved in the app's storage on the device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition – only when you start voice entry. Speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service on your phone (usually Google). The app saves only the text you confirm as an entry.
  • Fingerprint / face – if you turn on the lock. The check is done by the phone itself; the app doesn't receive biometric data.
  • Notifications – reminders (warranties, recurring entries, daily reminder) are scheduled locally on the device.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app signs in with your Google account and saves a copy of your data in a hidden app folder on your own Google Drive (“drive.appdata” access). The app can't see any other files on your Drive. The backup goes directly from your phone to Google's servers; we have no access to it. Your Google account's email address is used only for display in the app. The use of Google data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purchases

The premium version is bought through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; we don't receive your card details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of whether the purchase is active.

App updates

The app periodically checks for new code versions through the Expo service (Expo Updates). This sends technical data needed to deliver updates (e.g. app version and platform), but not your financial data.

Sharing with others

We don't share or sell your data. Only you share data, when you choose to export (Excel/CSV, PDF, backup) or send a reminder.

Deleting data

  • Data on the phone is deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data in Android settings.
  • Delete Google Drive backups in the app (by turning off the backup and removing access) or on Google Drive: Settings → Manage apps → Novko → Delete hidden app data.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
